# Focus Guard (Comment Verdict) — Chrome Web Store Summary

## One‑liner
Get a quick “should I watch this?” read on YouTube videos using comment signals — with Video Trust, Channel Trust, Comment Mood, and viewer-friendly takeaways.

## Short Description (Chrome Store–style)
Focus Guard helps you judge a YouTube video faster by summarizing what viewers are reacting to. It highlights a Video Trust score, a Channel Trust view, the overall Comment Mood, and key takeaways — all in a calm, non-judgmental tone.

## What It Does (Features)
- **Video Trust**: A simple trust score plus an easy-to-read verdict (e.g., “LEGIT”, “MISLEADING”, “CLICKBAIT”) to help you decide whether to keep watching.
- **Channel Trust**: A channel-level trust view that breaks down contributing factors in a transparent way.
- **Comment Mood**: A quick snapshot of how comments “feel” overall (Liked / Mixed / Disliked) with examples when available.
- **What Viewers Noticed**: Highlights the most common patterns and observations people are making in the comments.
- **Unaddressed Viewer Requests**: Surfaces what viewers are still asking for or wishing the video covered.
- **Summary tab**: A clear plain‑language summary and “Key Takeaways From Comments”.
- **Progress you can see**: While analysis runs, the toggle shows progress percent and a live progress message (so it doesn’t feel stuck).
- **On-page Side Panel**: Opens alongside YouTube so you can keep watching while reading the analysis.
- **Pre‑watch heads‑up**: A quick preview-style panel can appear after analysis completes (useful before you commit time).
- **Downloads (when available)**: Download a report for a video in supported formats.

## What It’s Based On
- Primarily **YouTube comment patterns** (plus available metadata/transcript signals depending on what’s available for the video).

## Tone & Expectations
- The extension is designed to be **helpful and tolerant** — it aims to summarize audience reaction and credibility signals without attacking creators or viewers.
- Results are **decision support**, not a guarantee. Treat them as an extra lens, not the only source of truth.

## Permissions (plain language)
- **YouTube site access**: Needed to run the analysis UI on YouTube watch pages.
- **Storage**: Used to save settings and keep the experience consistent.
